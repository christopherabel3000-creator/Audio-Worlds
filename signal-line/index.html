How to play

Drive your train for 90 seconds and go as far as you can. Never pass a signal while it's red.

  • Up arrow: more power (two steps to full power)
  • Down arrow: less power, then brake (two steps of brake below coast)
  • S: speed. D: distance and color of the next signal. T: time left.
  • Enter: start or restart

Sounds

  • The next signal sends a beacon that gets louder and faster as you get closer. High ping means green, double beep means yellow, low buzz means red.
  • When the next signal changes color you hear a short chime: rising for green, two even notes for yellow, falling for red.
  • A bell rings each time you clear a signal.
  • The engine and wheel clacks speed up as you go faster. Brakes squeal while braking.

Yellow lasts about 4 seconds, and at full speed the train needs about 6 seconds to stop on full brake. Listen ahead!
