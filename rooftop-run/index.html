How to play

Get from the ground floor of the first building to the roof of the fourth building in 90 seconds. If you die, you go back to the start, but the clock keeps running.

  1. The hallway. Walk right toward the ladder. Gunmen stand in doorways along the way. Each gunman's shot only covers the spot right in front of him, so don't be standing there when he fires.
  2. The ladder. When you reach it, climb with three presses of the up arrow. You can't walk left or right while you're on the ladder.
  3. The roof. A helicopter flies back and forth over the roof. Jump as it comes over you, then grab it. It carries you to the roof of the next building. Bombs fall now and then, so step out of the way.

Keys

  • Left arrow and Right arrow: walk. Tap for one step, or hold to keep walking.
  • Up arrow: climb the ladder. Down arrow: climb back down.
  • Space: jump. F: grab the helicopter while you're in the air.
  • P: where you are. T: time left. Enter: start or restart.

Sounds

  • Everything is placed left or right of you, so a sound in your right ear is ahead of you.
  • Gunmen: a click and a rising whine mean a gunman is about to fire. A whine in the center means he's aiming right at you, so move.
  • Ladder: it gives off a soft metal ping so you can find it.
  • Helicopter: it gets louder and brighter as it gets close, and it's directly over you when it's in the center and loudest.
  • Bombs: a falling whistle that drops in pitch, placed where the bomb will land. The blast also covers one step on each side of that spot.
