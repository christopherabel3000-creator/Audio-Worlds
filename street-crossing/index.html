How to play

Cross to the far side and back four times in 30 seconds without getting hit. The road has two lanes on each side of a median. The curbs and the median are safe; the lanes are not.

  • Up arrow: step toward the far side. Down arrow: step back toward the near side. Tap for one step, or hold to keep walking.
  • Three steps take you from a curb to the median, three more to the other curb.
  • P: where you are. T: time left. Enter: start or restart.

Sounds

  • Cars in the two near lanes drive from left to right. Cars in the two far lanes drive from right to left. You hear each car move across your speakers.
  • The closer a car's lane is to you, the louder it is. Each lane has its own engine pitch, with the lowest pitch nearest the near curb and the highest nearest the far curb.
  • Footsteps sound hard on the road and soft on the curbs and median. A chime plays when you reach the median, a bell at the far side, and a fanfare when you finish a round trip.
